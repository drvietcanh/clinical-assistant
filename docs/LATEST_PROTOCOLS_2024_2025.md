## 🫁 CRITICAL CARE PROTOCOLS

### 1. **SCCM PADIS 2025 Update**
**Pain, Agitation, Delirium, Immobility, Sleep Disruption**

**Published:** March 2025  
**Organization:** Society of Critical Care Medicine (SCCM)

**Key Recommendations:**
- ✅ **Dexmedetomidine over Propofol** for sedation (conditional)
- ✅ **Enhanced Mobilization/Rehabilitation** programs
- ✅ **Melatonin Administration** for sleep disruption
- ✅ **Multimodal Pain Management** approach
- ✅ **Delirium Prevention** strategies

**Implementation:**
```
1. Sedation Protocol:
   - First-line: Dexmedetomidine
   - Alternative: Propofol (if contraindications)
   - Target: RASS -2 to 0

2. Mobilization:
   - Start within 24-48 hours
   - Progressive rehabilitation
   - Multidisciplinary team approach

3. Sleep Hygiene:
   - Melatonin 3-5mg at bedtime
   - Reduce nighttime interruptions
   - Natural light exposure during day
```

### 3. **SCCM Heat Stroke Treatment 2024**

**Published:** 2024  
**Organization:** SCCM

**Key Recommendations:**
- ✅ **Rapid Cooling** to <39°C within 30 minutes
- ✅ **Cold Water Immersion** preferred method
- ✅ **Avoid Antipyretics** (ineffective)
- ✅ **Monitor for Complications** (DIC, rhabdomyolysis, AKI)

**Implementation:**
```
1. Immediate Actions:
   - Remove from heat source
   - Remove clothing
   - Start cooling immediately

2. Cooling Methods (in order):
   - Cold water immersion (14-17°C)
   - Evaporative cooling + fans
   - Ice packs to groin/axilla/neck

3. Monitoring:
   - Core temperature q15min
   - Stop cooling at 38.5°C
   - Labs: CBC, CMP, CK, coags
```

## ❤️ CARDIOLOGY PROTOCOLS

### 4. **2025 ACC/AHA Acute Coronary Syndromes**

**Published:** 2025  
**Organizations:** ACC/AHA/ACEP/NAEMSP/SCAI

**Key Updates:**
- ✅ **Early Risk Stratification** with hs-Troponin
- ✅ **Rapid Rule-Out** protocols (0/1-hour algorithm)
- ✅ **DAPT Duration** individualized approach
- ✅ **P2Y12 Inhibitor** selection based on risk
- ✅ **Early Invasive Strategy** for NSTEMI

**Implementation:**
```
1. STEMI Protocol:
   - Door-to-balloon: <90 minutes
   - Aspirin 325mg + P2Y12 inhibitor
   - Anticoagulation (heparin/bivalirudin)
   - Primary PCI preferred

2. NSTEMI Protocol:
   - hs-Troponin at 0 and 1 hour
   - GRACE score risk stratification
   - Early invasive (<24h) if high risk
   - Ticagrelor or Prasugrel preferred

3. Unstable Angina:
   - Serial troponins
   - Stress testing if low risk
   - Medical management
```

### 5. **2025 AHA/ACC Hypertension Guidelines**

**Published:** 2025  
**Organizations:** AHA/ACC

**Key Updates:**
- ✅ **New Screening Recommendations**
- ✅ **Lifestyle Modifications** emphasized
- ✅ **BP Targets** individualized
- ✅ **Home BP Monitoring** encouraged
- ✅ **Combination Therapy** early use

**Implementation:**
```
1. Diagnosis:
   - Office BP ≥130/80 mmHg
   - Confirm with home BP monitoring
   - ABPM if white coat suspected

2. Treatment Targets:
   - General: <130/80 mmHg
   - Elderly (>65): <130/80 if tolerated
   - CKD/DM: <130/80 mmHg

3. First-Line Therapy:
   - ACE-I/ARB + CCB or Thiazide
   - Consider combination pill
   - Lifestyle modifications always
```

## 🚨 EMERGENCY MEDICINE PROTOCOLS

### 7. **Sepsis Management 2024-2025**
**Restrictive Fluids + Early Vasopressors**

**Evidence:** Multiple 2024 trials

**Key Changes:**
- ✅ **Restrictive Fluid Strategy** (vs liberal)
- ✅ **Early Vasopressors** (within 1 hour)
- ✅ **Avoid Fluid Overload**
- ✅ **Balanced Crystalloids** preferred

**Implementation:**
```
1. Initial Resuscitation:
   - Crystalloid 500-1000mL bolus
   - Reassess after each bolus
   - Target MAP ≥65 mmHg

2. Vasopressor Initiation:
   - Start if MAP <65 after 1-2L fluids
   - Norepinephrine first-line
   - Peripheral access acceptable initially

3. Fluid Management:
   - Avoid >3L in first 3 hours
   - Use dynamic assessments
   - Monitor for fluid overload
```

### 8. **Trauma Resuscitation 2024**
**Whole Blood Resuscitation**

**Published:** 2024  
**Organization:** EAST (Eastern Association for Surgery of Trauma)

**Key Recommendation:**
- ✅ **Low-Titer Type O Whole Blood** for massive transfusion

**Implementation:**
```
1. Massive Transfusion Protocol:
   - Activate MTP early
   - Use whole blood if available
   - Alternative: 1:1:1 ratio (PRBC:FFP:Platelets)

2. Whole Blood Advantages:
   - Simplified logistics
   - Reduced donor exposures
   - Better hemostasis
   - Faster administration

3. Tranexamic Acid:
   - Give within 3 hours of injury
   - 1g IV over 10 min, then 1g over 8h
   - Earlier is better
```

### 10. **Severe Pneumonia 2024**
**Steroids for Mortality Reduction**

**Evidence:** 2024 meta-analyses

**Key Recommendation:**
- ✅ **Corticosteroids** reduce mortality in severe pneumonia

**Implementation:**
```
1. Indications:
   - Severe CAP (ICU admission)
   - Mechanical ventilation
   - High inflammatory markers

2. Regimen:
   - Methylprednisolone 40mg IV q12h x 5 days
   - OR Hydrocortisone 50mg IV q6h x 7 days
   - Taper over 3-5 days

3. Monitoring:
   - Blood glucose
   - Electrolytes
   - Secondary infections
```
