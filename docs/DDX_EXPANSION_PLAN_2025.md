## 🎯 Kế Hoạch Mở Rộng - Phase 1 (8 Scenarios Mới)

### **Priority 1: Emergency & Critical Care** 🔥🔥🔥

#### **1. Seizure (Động Kinh)** 
**Priority:** 🔥🔥🔥 CRITICAL  
**Clinical Value:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐  
**Diagnoses (5-6):**
- Generalized tonic-clonic seizure
- Focal seizure
- Status epilepticus
- Syncope (vs seizure)
- Psychogenic non-epileptic seizure (PNES)
- Hypoglycemia-induced seizure

**Key Features:**
- Duration, aura, postictal state
- Rule-out: Status epilepticus (emergency)
- Workup: EEG, CT/MRI, metabolic panel

**Estimated Time:** 1-1.5 hours

---

#### **2. Acute Confusion / Delirium (Lú Lẫn Cấp)**
**Priority:** 🔥🔥🔥 CRITICAL  
**Clinical Value:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐  
**Diagnoses (6-7):**
- Delirium (infectious, metabolic, toxic)
- Encephalitis
- Meningitis
- Stroke
- Hypoglycemia
- Drug toxicity
- Wernicke encephalopathy

**Key Features:**
- Acute vs chronic
- Rule-out: Meningitis, encephalitis (emergency)
- CAM-ICU score integration

**Estimated Time:** 1.5-2 hours

---

#### **3. Acute Kidney Injury (AKI) - Expanded**
**Priority:** 🔥🔥 HIGH  
**Clinical Value:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐  
**Note:** Đã có "Kidney Injury" nhưng có thể mở rộng thêm

**Diagnoses (5-6):**
- Prerenal AKI
- Intrinsic renal (ATN, GN, AIN)
- Postrenal (obstruction)
- Contrast-induced nephropathy
- Hepatorenal syndrome
- Cardiorenal syndrome

**Estimated Time:** 1-1.5 hours

### **Priority 2: Common Presentations** 🔥🔥

#### **4. Jaundice (Vàng Da)**
**Priority:** 🔥🔥 HIGH  
**Clinical Value:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐  
**Diagnoses (5-6):**
- Hepatitis (viral, alcoholic, drug-induced)
- Biliary obstruction
- Hemolytic anemia
- Gilbert syndrome
- Cholestasis
- Cirrhosis

**Key Features:**
- Direct vs indirect bilirubin
- Rule-out: Biliary obstruction (emergency)
- Workup: LFTs, imaging

**Estimated Time:** 1-1.5 hours

---

#### **5. Lymphadenopathy (Hạch To)**
**Priority:** 🔥🔥 HIGH  
**Clinical Value:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐  
**Diagnoses (5-6):**
- Reactive lymphadenopathy
- Infection (viral, bacterial, TB)
- Lymphoma
- Metastatic cancer
- Autoimmune (SLE, RA)
- Drug reaction

**Key Features:**
- Localized vs generalized
- Rule-out: Lymphoma, malignancy
- Workup: CBC, imaging, biopsy

**Estimated Time:** 1-1.5 hours

---

#### **6. Palpitations (Đánh Trống Ngực)**
**Priority:** 🔥🔥 HIGH  
**Clinical Value:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐  
**Diagnoses (5-6):**
- Atrial fibrillation
- SVT
- PVCs
- Anxiety/Panic disorder
- Hyperthyroidism
- Anemia

**Key Features:**
- Regular vs irregular
- Rule-out: Arrhythmias (emergency)
- Workup: ECG, Holter, thyroid function

**Estimated Time:** 1-1.5 hours

---

### **Priority 3: Specialty-Specific** 🔥

#### **7. Acute Limb Weakness (Yếu Chi Cấp)**
**Priority:** 🔥 HIGH  
**Clinical Value:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐  
**Diagnoses (5-6):**
- Stroke (ischemic, hemorrhagic)
- Transient ischemic attack (TIA)
- Spinal cord compression
- Guillain-Barré syndrome
- Myasthenia gravis
- Bell's palsy

**Key Features:**
- Unilateral vs bilateral
- Rule-out: Stroke (emergency - time-sensitive)
- Workup: CT/MRI, LP, EMG

**Estimated Time:** 1.5-2 hours

---

#### **8. Weight Loss (Sụt Cân)**
**Priority:** 🔥 MEDIUM  
**Clinical Value:** ⭐⭐⭐  
**Diagnoses (5-6):**
- Malignancy
- Hyperthyroidism
- Depression
- Diabetes (uncontrolled)
- Malabsorption
- Chronic infection (TB, HIV)

**Key Features:**
- Intentional vs unintentional
- Rule-out: Malignancy
- Workup: Comprehensive metabolic panel, imaging

**Estimated Time:** 1 hour
